The 9.2 kWh Illusion Explained

Your 2020 Kona Electric was built with ~67.5 kWh Gross physical capacity and 64.0 kWh Usable driving capacity.

Factory Overhead Buffer: ~3.5 kWh

The top 5.5% and bottom 3% are locked by Hyundai to stop cell charging at 4.16V (instead of 4.25V). This prevents cathode cracking and gave you 176,874 km of life!

Why 9.2 kWh is not degraded range:

Subtracting current 58.30 kWh from 67.50 kWh gross lumps together the 3.5 kWh factory reserve (which you never had access to) with your actual 5.70 kWh usable wear.
